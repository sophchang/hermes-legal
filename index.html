Hermes

A personal AI assistant used by a single user.

Hermes is a private, self-hosted assistant that helps its owner manage everyday tasks — reading her own email, keeping her calendar up to date, and searching her own cloud documents. It is not a public service: only the account owner can authorise it, and only her own data is accessed.

The application uses Google OAuth to access the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive with her explicit consent. No data is shared with third parties, and access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
