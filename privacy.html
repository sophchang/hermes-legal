Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This application ("Hermes") is a private, self-hosted personal assistant used by a single individual. This policy explains what data it accesses and how it is handled.

1. Who this applies to

Hermes is used only by its owner, who authorises it through Google OAuth with her own Google Account. There are no other users and no public sign-up.

2. Data accessed

  • Gmail — to read, search and send email on the owner's behalf, at her request.
  • Google Calendar — to read and create calendar events at her request.
  • Google Drive / Docs / Sheets — to find and read files the owner asks about.
  • Contacts — to look up people the owner mentions.

3. How data is used and stored

Data is used solely to carry out the owner's requests. It is processed on a private server controlled by the owner. OAuth tokens are stored locally on that server and are never sold, rented or shared with third parties.

4. Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, except when the owner explicitly asks the assistant to send a message or create an event that includes other people.

5. Retention

Message and calendar content is not retained beyond what is needed to answer a request and to keep the conversation history the owner can review and delete.

6. Revoking access

The owner can revoke this application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops all data access.
